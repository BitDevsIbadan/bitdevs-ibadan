# Code of Conduct

BitDevs Ibadan is a space for learning. People come with different levels of experience, and the Socratic format only works when everyone feels safe asking basic questions and saying "I don't know."

This applies at meetups, in our online channels, and in this repository.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Argue with ideas, not people.
- Let others finish speaking and share the floor.
- Respect privacy: ask before photographing someone or quoting them by name.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory jokes or language.
- Unwanted photography, recording, or physical contact.
- Shilling tokens, soliciting investment, or promoting scams.
- Sharing someone's private information without their permission.

## Reporting

If something happens, talk to an organizer at the meetup or on X **https://x.com/Abisuwa_Dunsin**. Reports are kept confidential.

Organizers may warn, remove, or ban anyone who breaks these rules, at meetups.
